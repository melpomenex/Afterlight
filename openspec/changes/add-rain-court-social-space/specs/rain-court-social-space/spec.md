## Purpose

Defines Rain Court as a complete navigable multiplayer social destination with distinct composition, reusable atmosphere and measurable acceptance.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Stable Rain Court destination

Rain Court SHALL use existing court identity, preserve its legacy exits and save recognition, and remain objective-free with no field-note requirement.

#### Scenario: Old link

- **WHEN** a visitor opens room=court
- **THEN** the new Rain Court appears under the same ID with existing saved status intact.

### Requirement: Distinct navigable rain square

The place SHALL include an open rain square, north covered arcade, sheltered alcove, rain-chain basin visual landmark and at least six usable seats across three gathering zones. Player/Kiln spawns, gates and seat dismounts SHALL be collision-safe and reachable.

#### Scenario: Walk the destination

- **WHEN** a visitor enters with Kiln and visits all gathering areas
- **THEN** clear routes connect each area and all exits without walking through substantial props.

#### Scenario: Use alcove seat

- **WHEN** a visitor sits then stands in the alcove
- **THEN** orientation matches the bench and dismount returns to walkable ground.

### Requirement: Convincing spatial rain

The place SHALL show falling rain, wet exposed paving, bounded puddles, amber lamps against cool fill, sheltered precipitation masking and distinct exposed/roof/alcove audio. Lightning/thunder SHALL obey shared timing and local comfort settings.

#### Scenario: Move under cover

- **WHEN** a visitor walks from square to arcade and alcove
- **THEN** rain below roofs reduces, outside rain remains visible and the audio becomes progressively sheltered.

#### Scenario: Heavy rain late join

- **WHEN** a second visitor arrives during heavy rain
- **THEN** both observe the same semantic weather and future lightning schedule without replaying earlier thunder.

### Requirement: Rain Court acceptance

The place SHALL satisfy its specified draw/triangle/light and shared effect budgets at normal/reduced quality, with four-camera evidence and repeated-travel resource checks.

#### Scenario: Acceptance run

- **WHEN** the busiest rain/event state is exercised with seated visitors
- **THEN** the report includes real measurements, shelter/seat screenshots and zero hidden-world effect updates.
